# Privacy Policy - TabSync AI

**Last updated:** August 1, 2026

TabSync AI ("we", "our") is committed to protecting user privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when using the TabSync AI Chrome extension.

### 1. Data Collection and Use

To provide its functionality, TabSync AI accesses the following data locally within the browser:

- **Open tab URLs and titles:** Used exclusively for AI-powered categorization and grouping.
- **Bookmark structure and tab groups:** Used exclusively for cleaning saved groups and workspace restoration.

### 2. Data Processing and Third Parties

- **No data selling:** We do not sell, rent, or trade user browsing data with third parties for marketing or advertising.
- **AI Processing (OpenRouter API):** During automatic organization, tab URLs and titles are sent anonymously and encrypted to our backend for LLM processing. No browsing data is permanently stored on AI servers.
- **Payments (Stripe):** Credit purchase transactions are handled entirely by Stripe. TabSync AI does not store or access user credit card data.

### 3. Data Retention

Backup files (.json) reside exclusively on the user's local device or their personal Google Drive account (if enabled). The user has full control over deleting such files.

### 4. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: support@tabsync.ai
